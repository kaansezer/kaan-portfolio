# Design DNA: Kaan Sezer Portfolio

## Mission
Create a **premium, art-directed portfolio** that feels professionally crafted, not generic. Every visual decision is intentional. The design serves the content—engineering expertise and PCB/embedded systems work—with confident typography, deliberate whitespace, and refined interactions.

## Core Principles
- **Editorial Quality**: Confident typography with strong hierarchy
- **Intentional Asymmetry**: Deliberate imbalance creates visual interest
- **Strong Whitespace**: Breathing room between sections; never cramped
- **Subtle Depth**: Layering and shadows convey premium feel without gloss
- **Purposeful Motion**: Animations enhance, never distract
- **Authentic Tech Aesthetic**: Engineering-inspired, not generic SaaS

## Target User Experience
Professional → Impressed → Confident (in Kaan's abilities)

First 5 seconds: "This person is serious about their craft"
After scroll: "This is technically excellent and visually thoughtful"

## Design Language
- **Dark-first** (with light mode support)
- **Warm palette**: Green/teal background with orange/warm accents
- **Technical typography**: Mix of display serif (Fraunces), sans (Public Sans), mono (JetBrains)
- **Refined geometry**: 6px-12px rounded corners, consistent grid-based spacing
- **Engineering precision**: Grids, technical labels, layer stacks

## Anti-Patterns
- No excessive gradients or glowing effects
- No glassmorphism or neumorphism trends
- No repetitive card grids
- No generic animations everywhere
- No overused decorative icons
- No rounded rectangles everywhere ("pill" aesthetic)
